*24-11-19*

Today I bring you great news and a very nice surprise. In the implementation guidance draft proposal for NIS2, European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) has included a mapping table to standards and frameworks including the Spanish National Security Framework (ENS - RD 311/2022) as a reference for compliance. This is so proud, ENS is the facto an international reference.
